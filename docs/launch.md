## English introduction — draft

**Unlikely: one task, more than one way in**

I built Unlikely to explore creative ideas through connections. Start with something you want to make—a short film, a product, a business experiment—then explore a panoramic map, inspect why ideas connect, and choose your own route.

To get a feel for it, open one of three curated examples. They all start from lemon concentrate and lead toward different tasks. Six preset routes have complete authored outcomes; you can also choose, rearrange, and export your own route without a key.

For a custom task, connect your own compatible model directly in the browser. Enter the API base URL, model name, and key, test the connection, then generate a map. The key stays in page memory and clears on refresh. Requests go to your chosen provider; the connection test and subsequent model calls may incur charges on your account. If the provider blocks browser requests through CORS, there is a local-server option.

The integrations have mock-based tests, but no real provider has yet been verified. Curated examples and model drafts are labeled separately.

Try a route and tell me which connection surprised you—or felt forced. If you want to follow the project, a GitHub star is welcome.

[Try Unlikely](https://rre37826-source.github.io/unlikely/) · [Source](https://github.com/rre37826-source/unlikely)
