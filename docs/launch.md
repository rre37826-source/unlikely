## English introduction — draft

**Unlikely: explore unexpected connections, then choose your own creative route**

What could lemon concentrate have to do with a short film, a product for interrupted creative work, or a café experiment?

I built Unlikely to make that exploration visible. It starts with a concrete task and a panoramic map. Click a connection to read the reasoning, keep the ideas that interest you, and arrange them into a route. If a connection is missing, it stays visible as a gap; suggested paths through existing relationships need your approval.

The browser demo includes three bilingual, curated examples and six complete preset routes. You can edit routes and export Markdown, JSON, or an SVG card without an account or API key. Other routes are summarized faithfully; they are not presented as fresh AI proposals.

There is also an optional local adapter for OpenAI-compatible model endpoints to generate maps, expand nodes, and develop selected routes. It has mock-based tests, but I have not yet validated it against a real provider. The static demo does not make model calls.

Try one of the examples and tell me which connection surprised you—or felt forced. If you want to follow the project, a GitHub star is welcome.

- Demo: https://rre37826-source.github.io/unlikely/
- Code: https://github.com/rre37826-source/unlikely

## What to observe after publishing

Use the first launch to learn whether visitors understand and want the exploration, then improve the weakest step.

| Stage | Evidence to collect | What it can tell you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notice | Repository views and available referral totals over a stated date range; each post's visible reach and replies | Whether the example and positioning draw attention |
| Understand | Feedback from people trying the demo: can they explain a connection and choose a route? | Whether the interaction communicates its purpose |
| Use | Voluntarily shared route cards, example contributions, reproducible issues, and descriptions of actual tasks | What people want to make and where exploration breaks down |
| Follow | Net new repository stars, forks, and contributors during the same period | Overall interest in following or building on the project |

The current app has no analytics instrumentation. Do not report measured demo activation, export conversion, or retention unless a measurement method is added and disclosed. Aggregate referral counts, page views, and stars cannot identify which visitor later starred the repository. A ratio such as new stars divided by repository visitors is a coarse period-level observation, not a verified user funnel or proof that a particular post caused the stars.

Keep a simple dated record of posts, copy variants, observed totals, and qualitative feedback. Compare one change at a time when practical, but describe small or uncontrolled differences as observations rather than causal conclusions. Do not buy stars, automate endorsements, or promise rewards for starring.
