## English introduction — current draft

**Unlikely: one task, more than one way in**

What could lemon concentrate have to do with a short film, a product for interrupted work, or a café experiment?

I built Unlikely to explore those connections. Start with a task, see the full map, inspect why ideas connect, and choose the ones worth developing. Three curated examples and six complete preset routes are available now, without registration or model setup. You can choose your own route and export it as Markdown, JSON, or an SVG card.

The examples are authored, not live-generated. A public generation gateway is implemented but not activated while operator credentials, pricing, and budget setup are completed. Using your own compatible model remains an optional advanced path, with charges on your own provider account. No real provider is currently claimed as verified.

Try a route and tell me which connection surprised you—or felt forced. If you want to follow the project, a GitHub star is welcome.
